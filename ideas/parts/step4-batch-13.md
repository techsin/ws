### 301. Ex Post Facto — MMO: 5/10 | TOTAL: 15/30 | VERDICT: MINE

### 303. Built to Flip — MMO: 7/10 | TOTAL: 22/30 | VERDICT: KEEP

### 304. Spin Cycle — MMO: 4/10 | TOTAL: 14/30 | VERDICT: MINE
