### 376. Ring Trade — MMO: 7/10 | TOTAL: 21/30 | VERDICT: MINE
Structure: The want-graph is dense enough for loops at 10,000 players but thins to near-barren at 400, and the private-map social layer is genuinely the engine — yet the whole game is scout-work with no venture underneath, so it reads as a superb subsystem rather than a server.
Exploit: Alt accounts posting bait wants to trap rivals into holding worthless intermediates, plus Discord loop-map cartels — the cartel half IS the alpha-decay lesson, but the bait-alt half just teaches paranoia about the developer's spawn tables.
Steal: The loop-close-as-timed-heist — once you commit to closing a multi-leg chain, every handshake is a live beat-the-clock sequence and your margin evaporates the moment the route becomes public knowledge.

### 377. Caravan Divergence — MMO: 3/10 | TOTAL: 13/30 | VERDICT: KILL

### 378. Recipe Shadow — MMO: 7/10 | TOTAL: 21/30 | VERDICT: MINE

Exploit: A crafter's supplier-alt front-runs its own recipe pivot to corner ingredients before going public — insider trading that is realistic but lets a two-account player skip the entire inference game the design exists to teach.

### 379. The Spread — MMO: 4/10 | TOTAL: 15/30 | VERDICT: MINE

### 380. Shelf Wars — MMO: 3/10 | TOTAL: 14/30 | VERDICT: MINE

### 381. Rot Ledger — MMO: 6/10 | TOTAL: 20/30 | VERDICT: MINE

### 382. Handshake Futures — MMO: 9/10 | TOTAL: 26/30 | VERDICT: KEEP

### 385. The Dumb Ledger — MMO: 4/10 | TOTAL: 17/30 | VERDICT: MINE
