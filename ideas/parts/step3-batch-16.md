### 376. Ring Trade — FUN: 7/10
Hook: The eureka of snapping a five-leg loop closed and skimming every leg is a genuine puzzle-solve high players will diagram and brag about.
Poison: Most sessions are trudging through stale and bait listings, and the loop closing without you overnight punishes people for sleeping.
Fix: Make loop execution a live timed sequence — once you start closing, every leg is a beat-the-clock handshake — so the payoff is a heist, not a mailbox check.

### 377. Caravan Divergence — FUN: 5/10
Hook: Rolling into a distant gate and watching the price board reveal is a real slot-machine moment — did the gap survive or did fifty rivals beat you here?
Poison: The verb between reveals is hauling, the most notoriously dead time in MMO history, and third-party price tools will kill even the reveal.
Fix: Compress hauls to 10-minute active runs with route hazards and choices, so a session is depart-decide-arrive-reveal instead of an evening of walking.

### 378. Recipe Shadow — FUN: 6/10
Hook: Correctly deducing a secret recipe from purchase patterns and cornering the ingredient before the fad peaks is real detective satisfaction with a jackpot attached.
Poison: Hour 100 is surveillance spreadsheets and slow feedback — the design has one great verb (infer) executed mostly by staring at sales logs.
Fix: Add active intelligence verbs — bribable apprentices, tavern eavesdropping, sample-lot stings — so deduction is fieldwork with partial leaks, not passive chart-watching.

### 379. The Spread — FUN: 6/10
Hook: Surviving a week of quotes without getting gutted, then autopsying the run that almost killed you, is white-knuckle poker for finance-brained players.
Poison: Standing quotes demand constant vigilance — the game punishes logging off, a lifestyle only a niche will accept and everyone else will churn from.
Fix: Bound the exchange to scheduled 90-minute market sessions so your quotes only live while you're present and each open is an appointment thriller.

### 380. Shelf Wars — FUN: 5/10
Hook: Slot auction night — outbidding a rival landlord for the corner stall, or watching a scalper eat rent on a suddenly dead aisle — is delicious public drama.
Poison: With 200 slots and 10,000 players, 9,800 people are spectators to an incumbent landlord oligopoly they can never crack.
Fix: Rotate the bazaar seasonally — new districts open, old ones decay, all leases expire — so slot value resets and every player gets a land-rush moment.

### 381. Rot Ledger — FUN: 7/10
Hook: The dawn auction into evening fire-sale arc is a perfect Animal-Crossing-turnip daily ritual, and dumping fish at half price while rivals circle is genuinely theatrical.
Poison: Hidden variable spoilage reads as unfair RNG, and a literal dawn auction is a lifestyle demand that punishes anyone with a job in the wrong timezone.
Fix: Compress a full market day into one 45-minute session with staggered openings per region, so the buy-spoil-dump arc fits any evening and decay never runs while you're gone.

### 382. Handshake Futures — FUN: 8/10
Hook: This is EVE's proven fun engine — legendary defaults, player courts, escrow moguls, and betrayal stories that get retold for years are the content.
Poison: The median new player's first month is being farmed by scammers with zero recourse, which is misery-without-catharsis for everyone but the wolves.

### 383. Cap Table — FUN: 5/10

Poison: The core loop for most players is posting pitches into a silent feed, which is LinkedIn with extra steps and no verbs.

### 384. Mintmakers — FUN: 6/10
Hook: Watching your coin's adoption chart collapse on an untraceable rumor — or engineering a rival's hyperinflation via whisper campaign — is crypto-Twitter chaos as a game, and chaos is content.
Poison: 99% of issued coins die in silence, so most players get a central-banker fantasy that nobody ever shows up to.

### 385. The Dumb Ledger — FUN: 5/10

### 386. Allocation Day — FUN: 7/10

### 387. Brownout — FUN: 4/10

### 388. Standard Bearers — FUN: 4/10

### 389. Built to Last — FUN: 4/10

### 390. The Last Shoal — FUN: 5/10

### 391. Honesty Box — FUN: 5/10

### 392. Taste — FUN: 7/10

### 393. Payroll — FUN: 5/10
